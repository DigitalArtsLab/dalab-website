LAB VISION

The Digital Arts Lab explores the intersection of creative practice and digital technology. We investigate how emerging media shapes human experience, bridging the gap between artistic inquiry and technological innovation.

Our work is driven by the belief that design and art are essential to understanding the digital future. This vision is built upon two core research areas. Both share a common foundation: a focus on design quality, artistic research, and the systematic examination of the aesthetic and experiential dimensions of digital media.

RESEARCH FOCUS

GAMES &
INTERACTION DESIGN

Investigating playful systems, meaningful interaction paradigms, and the psychological impact of game mechanics on user behavior. We design and analyze novel interfaces that challenge traditional modes of interaction.

RESEARCH FOCUS

ANIMATION &
INTERACTIVE ART

Exploring motion graphics, generative visuals, and artistic applications of real-time computer graphics in virtual spaces. Our focus lies on immersive storytelling and aesthetic experiences in XR environments.
